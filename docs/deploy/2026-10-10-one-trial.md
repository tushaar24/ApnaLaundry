# Deploy: one ₹2 trial per shop (2026-10-10)

Before, every new subscription got the ₹2 trial + 7 free days, so a shop whose
trial ended unpaid (halted) or was cancelled saw the ₹2 offer again and could
repeat it forever. Now the trial is once per shop: after that the plan is billed
in full the moment the shop approves UPI AutoPay.

- **Used the trial** = any subscription of the shop that got past checkout
  (status other than `created` / `abandoned`) — including cancelled, halted,
  completed. A checkout that was never approved doesn't use it up.
- **Backend** (`routes/laundry-billing.js`): `/subscribe` adds the ₹2 addon +
  `start_at +7d` only when eligible; otherwise Razorpay charges the plan on
  approval. A pending `created` ₹2 attempt is only reused if the trial terms
  still match. `/status` returns `trialEligible` and `plans.trial.amount: 0`
  when the trial is used.
- **Web + Android paywall**: when `trialEligible` is false — "Your free trial
  has ended · Pick a plan", "Choose your plan", "Pays ₹X today, then every
  month/year", CTA "PAY ₹X & CONTINUE", matching FAQ and retry-sheet copy.

**Old Android builds** (before this change) don't know `trialEligible`; for a
shop that already used its trial they show "₹0 trial" copy, but Razorpay
Checkout shows the real full amount before approval. Ship the Android update.

Order: **backend**, then **website**, then the next Play build.
